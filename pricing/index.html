How the cost per 1,000 views works

A campaign’s rate is the price for each 1,000 approved views. For example, at $0.75 per 1,000 views, one million approved views would equal $750 at that rate, subject to the campaign budget and terms.

Estimate a campaign using the rate

The arithmetic for a rate-based campaign is simple: divide approved views by 1,000, then multiply by the campaign’s rate. At $0.75 per 1,000 views, 100,000 approved views would equal $75 at that rate; one million approved views would equal $750. These are examples of the calculation, not a forecast or a promise that a campaign will reach those view totals.

The actual total remains subject to the campaign budget, the views approved under its rules, and the terms shown before launch. The minimum campaign is $1,000. Rates begin at $0.75 per 1,000 approved views and may change later. Launch pricing is currently $0.

What to compare before choosing a campaign service

Headline rates can describe different things. Before comparing two offers, check whether the quoted amount is paid per approved view, per post, or through another model. Then compare what work is included, how views are reviewed, which platforms are allowed, and whether the offer has a budget or payout limit.

  • Read the rate and the definition of an approved view.
  • Check how invalid or botted views are handled.
  • Confirm the minimum budget and any campaign limits.
  • Review the brief, publishing requirements, and what the brand controls.
  • Use the current written terms; a past or third-party rate may no longer apply.

Other services use different campaign models and rates, so a single market-wide comparison can be misleading. Check each provider’s current published terms before comparing.
