Frequently asked questions

ClipRise is new. Why should I trust it?

Honest answer: we are new and haven’t run campaigns for external clients yet. What we can offer is transparency: a public rate card, a documented 4-point QA standard, and an escrow-based payment model, so money only moves when work is approved. You can inspect our free Whop community before spending anything. We think that’s a more trustworthy starting point than unverifiable case studies.

How does ClipRise verify clip quality?

Every clip goes through our documented 4-point QA. Real views are approved and botted views are rejected before any payout.
