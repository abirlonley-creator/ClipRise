What to include in a campaign brief

A good brief gives editors enough direction to make consistent choices without guessing what the brand wants. Start with the purpose of the campaign and the audience you want the clips to speak to. Then describe the editing style, the platform requirements, the hashtags, the account mentions, and any other rules that matter to your brand.

  • Share the source footage and identify any sections that should not be used.
  • Describe the tone and editing style in plain language; examples can help explain the direction.
  • Name the intended audience and any platform-specific requirements.
  • Write hashtags, mentions, and required wording exactly as they should appear.
  • Confirm the campaign budget, rate, view-review rules, and other terms before launch.

The brief is the reference point throughout the campaign. If a requirement is important, put it in writing before the work starts. That gives the clipping community a consistent target and helps you review whether a post follows your instructions.
