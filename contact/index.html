Before you get in touch

For brands, it helps to have the source footage and a brief that describes the editing style, audience, hashtags, mentions, and other requirements. Campaign launch pricing is currently $0, the minimum campaign is $1,000, and rates start at $0.75 per 1,000 approved views. The exact rate and terms are shown before launch.

For clippers, review the campaign brief and payout terms before you begin. View approval is based on campaign review; real views are approved and botted or invalid views are rejected.

For questions about who operates the service, personal-data requests, or legal terms, use the official channels above and identify the page or campaign you are asking about.
