About ClipRise

The people and process behind ClipRise

ClipRise coordinates clipping campaigns for brands and creators, connecting a clear brief with editors who turn long videos into short-form posts.

Who runs ClipRise

ClipRise was co-founded by Abir Ahmed and Sakib. They bring different perspectives to the work: Abir focuses on marketing and campaign operations, while Sakib contributes hands-on clipping, search engine optimization, and content strategy experience.

The company’s role is to coordinate a campaign from its brief through review. A brand supplies raw footage and requirements. Clippers make and publish clips that follow those requirements. ClipRise reviews campaign views, approves real views, and rejects botted or invalid views.

The brief stays with the brand. It can set the editing style, audience, hashtags, mentions, and other campaign requirements. The campaign rate and terms are shown before launch. Launching is currently $0, the minimum campaign is $1,000, and rates start at $0.75 per 1,000 approved views.

How views are checked

Views are reviewed before they count toward a campaign. ClipRise approves real views and rejects views identified as botted or invalid. The campaign’s rate applies to approved views under the terms shown before launch.

View checks are part of the campaign review, alongside whether a clip follows the brand’s brief. This lets the brand see which work is eligible for approval without treating every reported view as valid automatically.

For details about a particular campaign, review its written brief and terms before it launches. See campaign pricing or contact ClipRise.
