Sakib — Co-Founder of ClipRise

Sakib is the co-founder of ClipRise, bringing together hands-on clipping experience, search engine optimization (SEO), and content strategy to help brands and creators reach wider audiences. Having worked with more than 900 clients around the world, Sakib has developed a deep understanding of the content landscape and the strategies that help videos stand out in an increasingly competitive digital environment.

Before co-founding ClipRise, Sakib worked as a clipper himself, gaining firsthand experience in turning long-form content into short-form videos designed to capture attention. From identifying compelling moments to understanding pacing, storytelling, and audience engagement, his experience behind the editing process gave him a practical understanding of what makes a clip worth watching. He knows that successful short-form content is not simply about cutting a video down — it is about finding the right moment, delivering the message effectively, and creating something that makes people want to keep watching.

His work with clients across different markets also helped him develop a broader perspective on content performance. Every creator, brand, and campaign has its own audience, objectives, and challenges. Through these experiences, Sakib learned the importance of adapting content strategies to different goals rather than relying on a one-size-fits-all approach.

Beyond clipping, Sakib brings expertise in SEO and content strategy, allowing him to look at content from another important perspective: discoverability. Creating a compelling video is only part of the process. Understanding how content is found, how audiences discover it, and how it can be positioned for greater visibility is equally important. His knowledge of SEO helps him think beyond the edit itself, considering the broader factors that influence how content reaches its intended audience.

This combination of creative experience and strategic thinking shapes the way Sakib approaches content growth. He understands the journey from the initial editing process to the moment a video appears in front of a viewer. By connecting these different parts of the process, he aims to help brands and creators make smarter decisions about their content and distribution strategies.

At ClipRise, Sakib applies this experience to help clients maximize the potential of their content through strategic clipping and organic reach. His goal is to help brands turn existing videos, interviews, podcasts, and other long-form material into opportunities for increased visibility and audience engagement. Rather than treating content creation and distribution as separate challenges, he believes they work best when they are approached as parts of the same overall strategy.

As co-founder, Sakib contributes a creator-first perspective to ClipRise. Having worked directly with clipping and content, he understands the effort required to produce videos consistently and the frustration of seeing good content struggle to gain attention. This firsthand experience helps him approach campaigns with a practical understanding of both the creative process and the challenges clients face when trying to grow online.

His experience working with 900+ clients worldwide has also reinforced the importance of communication, consistency, and understanding each client's specific goals. Sakib believes effective content strategy starts with knowing what a client wants to achieve and identifying the best way to help their content reach the right audience. Whether the objective is increasing brand awareness, expanding a creator's audience, or improving content discoverability, his approach focuses on aligning strategy with the desired outcome.

Through ClipRise, Sakib is working toward making content distribution more strategic, accessible, and effective for businesses and creators. He wants to help clients move beyond simply publishing videos and toward building a more consistent approach to getting their content discovered. By combining clipping expertise, SEO knowledge, and content strategy, he brings a well-rounded perspective to the company's mission.

For Sakib, ClipRise is an opportunity to bring together the skills he has developed throughout his work in the content industry. It combines the creative side of making engaging clips with the strategic side of helping those clips get discovered and seen by more people.

With experience as a former clipper, an SEO expert, a content strategist, and a professional who has worked with more than 900 clients worldwide, Sakib brings practical knowledge and a growth-focused mindset to ClipRise. His mission is to help brands and creators get more value from their content by connecting great editing with smarter strategy and stronger distribution.

At ClipRise, Sakib helps connect the art of making great clips with the strategy of getting them seen.
