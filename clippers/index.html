A practical workflow for clippers

Start by reading the entire campaign brief before opening the footage. Check which platforms and accounts are named, what editing style the brand wants, and whether the campaign requires specific hashtags, mentions, or wording. If a detail is unclear, ask through the campaign’s official channel before publishing.

  1. Confirm the footage belongs to the campaign and use it only for that work.
  2. Choose a moment that fits the brief and keep the clip understandable without relying on missing context.
  3. Edit to the requested style and format; check captions, spelling, and any required brand mentions.
  4. Publish only where the campaign allows and follow the social platform’s rules.
  5. Keep the post link and review the campaign’s submission and payout terms.

Views are reviewed before they count. ClipRise approves real views and rejects botted or invalid views. A view total shown by a social platform is not automatically the same as an approved campaign total, so read each campaign’s review rules and wait for its review process.

Check the terms before you begin

Campaigns can differ in rate, platform, budget, and creative requirements. Review the written brief and payout terms before spending time editing. The rate per 1,000 views, how invalid views are treated, and any campaign limits should be clear in the terms shown to you.

ClipRise does not guarantee a particular number of views or earnings. The campaign’s final approved views and applicable terms determine what counts. Do not use bots, fake traffic, or engagement manipulation; these conflict with the view-review rules and may also violate platform requirements.
